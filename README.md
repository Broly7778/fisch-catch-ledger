## 🌊 What This Is

Fisch Catch Tracker — Companion Edition is a passive observation layer that sits quietly beside your Roblox session. It watches, it notes, it organizes. You fish. It remembers.

Think of it as the ship's log that a seasoned captain keeps — except this one never smudges, never forgets a page, and never argues about which lagoon held the rarest specimen.

Every catch becomes a data point. Every data point becomes a pattern. Every pattern becomes insight you can actually use the next time the tide turns.

---

## 💡 Why It Exists

Manual journals are noble. They are also tedious. Nobody wants to tab out mid-session to type "caught a shiny something-or-other near the eastern rocks around dusk." That moment is gone the second you look away.

This companion was born from a simple frustration: the best fishing stories are written in real time, but the tools to write them never kept up. So instead of asking you to pause, we built something that keeps pace.

The result is a tracker that respects your attention, your screen space, and your instincts.

## 🧩 Core Modules

### 1. The Auto-Recording Loop
Every catch event is captured the moment it resolves, with metadata attached: timestamp, location tag, rarity classification, and session context. No hotkeys, no confirmations, no interruptions.

### 2. The Catch Ledger
A structured, searchable archive of everything you have hauled aboard. Filter by rarity, region, time of day, or session. Sort by whatever matters to you in the moment.

### 3. The Environmental Awareness Engine
Pairs each catch with ambient conditions — weather cycles, in-game time, and regional modifiers — so you can correlate effort with outcome.

### 4. The Personal Museum
A showcase view that lets you curate your proudest specimens into a presentation-worthy collection. Ideal for sharing with friends or simply admiring on a quiet evening.

### 5. Session Summaries
After each play session, receive a compact digest: total catches, rarity distribution, most productive waters, and streaks worth celebrating.

## 📖 The Catch Ledger Explained

The ledger is the beating heart of this companion. It is not merely a list — it is a journal with structure.

Each entry contains:

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| Species | The name of what you pulled up |
| Rarity Tier | Common through Mythic, color-coded |
| Location | Region, zone, and approximate coordinates |
| Timestamp | Date, in-game time, and real-world moment |
| Conditions | Weather, tide state, and modifiers |
| Session ID | Links the entry to its parent session |
| Notes | Optional personal annotation field |

Searching is instant. Filtering is granular. Exporting is one step. Your history becomes a research library, not a pile of scattered notes.

## 🌦 Environmental Awareness Engine

Fishing is rarely random. The deep has rhythms.

This engine correlates your catches with the conditions surrounding them. Over time, patterns emerge — certain rarities appear more readily during particular weather cycles, or in specific zones after in-game dusk. The engine surfaces these correlations without pretending to be clairvoyant.

It does not tell you what to do. It shows you what has happened, and lets your instincts finish the thought.

## 📱 Responsive Interface Design

The tracker interface was designed around one principle: it should never demand more room than it deserves.

- On ultrawide displays, panels expand gracefully into a multi-column dashboard.
- On standard laptops, the layout compresses into a clean two-column arrangement.
- On smaller windows, it collapses into a single scrollable column without losing functionality.

No awkward clipping. No hidden controls. The design bends; it does not break.

## ⚙️ Performance Philosophy

A companion should be invisible until needed.

This tracker was engineered with a lightweight footprint in mind. It avoids aggressive polling, caches intelligently, and releases resources the moment they are no longer required. The result is a tool that coexists peacefully with your session rather than competing for its attention.

If you ever notice it, something has gone wrong.
